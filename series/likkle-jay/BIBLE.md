## Characters

### Likkle Jay

- Young male cartoon character; medium/dark brown skin; round youthful face
- **HAIR HARD LOCK:** rounded dome of tight black curls, full and consistent
- Never: fade, taper, crop differently, flatten, slick, change texture/silhouette/length

- Rounded/stout silhouette; medium/dark brown skin
- Orange/red floral house dress; matching patterned headwrap
